   C. Brain

   :correct: C
   :explanation: The nervous system is made up of the brain, spinal cord, and all the peripheral nerves that branch out to every part of the body.

   B. Brain and stomach
   C. Brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nerves

   :explanation: Neuroscience draws from biology, psychology, physics, computer science, medicine, and more, making it truly multidisciplinary.

   C. Many different fields of study

   B. Brain

.. mcq:: What does the spinal cord mainly do?
   :correct: C
   :explanation: The spinal cord acts as the main communication highway, relaying signals between the brain and the rest of the body.

   A. Pumps blood
   B. Helps digestion
   C. Carries signals between brain and body

.. mcq:: Nerves help in:
   :correct: A
   :explanation: Nerves are bundles of axons that carry electrical signals to and from the brain and spinal cord throughout the body.

   A. Carrying electrical signals
   B. Digesting food
   C. Cleaning blood
   D. Making bones

.. mcq:: What are biopotential signals?
   :correct: C
   :explanation: Biopotential signals are tiny electrical signals produced naturally by the body's cells, especially neurons and muscle cells, when they are active.

   A. Artificial computer signals
   B. Signals from satellites
   C. Tiny electrical signals produced by the body
   D. Sound waves from muscles

.. mcq:: Which biopotential signal is related to the brain?
   :correct: B
   :explanation: EEG (Electroencephalogram) measures the electrical activity of the brain, recorded from electrodes placed on the scalp.

   A. ECG
   B. EEG
   C. EMG
   D. EOG

.. mcq:: Which signal measures muscle activity?
   :correct: C
   :explanation: EMG (Electromyogram) records the electrical activity of skeletal muscles during contraction or rest.

   A. EEG
   B. ECG
   C. EMG
   D. EOG

.. mcq:: ECG is used to measure:
   :correct: D
   :explanation: ECG (Electrocardiogram) captures the electrical activity of the heart, showing the heart's rhythm and health.

   A. Eye movement
   B. Brain activity
   C. Muscle activity
   D. Heart activity

.. mcq:: EOG is mainly used to detect:
   :correct: B
   :explanation: EOG (Electrooculogram) detects the electrical potential difference between the cornea and retina of the eye, which changes as the eye moves.

   A. Heartbeats
   B. Eye movements
   C. Brain waves
   D. Muscle strength

.. mcq:: Which signal helps study meditation and sleep?
   :correct: B
   :explanation: EEG captures brain wave patterns (alpha, theta, delta waves) that change during meditation and different sleep stages.

   A. ECG
   B. EEG
   C. EMG
   D. EOG

.. mcq:: Which signal would best help detect if a person blinked?
   :correct: D
   :explanation: A blink produces a characteristic spike in the EOG signal because the movement of the eyelid changes the cornea-retina electrical potential.

   A. ECG
   B. EMG
   C. EEG
   D. EOG

Section 2: Arduino Basics & Electronics
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. mcq:: Which Arduino board is most popular for beginners?
   :correct: A
   :explanation: The Arduino Uno is the most widely used beginner board due to its simplicity, large community, and abundant learning resources.

   A. Arduino Uno
   B. Arduino Nano Every 33 IoT
   C. Raspberry Pi
   D. ESP8266

.. mcq:: If an LED connected to Arduino suddenly glows, what is MOST likely true?
   :correct: A
   :explanation: An LED glows when current flows through it. This happens when the connected pin is set HIGH, providing voltage and allowing current to flow through the LED.

   A. The pin became HIGH
   B. WiFi is connected
   C. The battery exploded
   D. The monitor is ON

.. mcq:: Which pin on Arduino is commonly used to provide power to sensors?
   :correct: C
   :explanation: The 5V pin provides a stable 5-volt power supply from the Arduino's onboard regulator, commonly used to power sensors and modules.

   A. GND
   B. RX
   C. 5V
   D. TX

.. mcq:: A student connects both wires of an LED to GND. What will happen?
   :correct: C
   :explanation: For current to flow through an LED, one wire must connect to a positive voltage (like 5V or a HIGH pin) and the other to GND. With both wires on GND, there is no potential difference, so no current flows and the LED stays off.

   A. LED becomes brighter
   B. LED turns ON
   C. LED stays OFF
   D. Arduino starts beeping

.. mcq:: Which function runs repeatedly in an Arduino program?
   :correct: B
   :explanation: The loop() function runs continuously after setup() completes. All ongoing tasks, reading sensors, sending data, checking conditions, go inside loop().

   A. setup()
   B. loop()
   C. start()
   D. begin()

.. mcq:: Which function runs only once after Arduino powers ON?
   :correct: C
   :explanation: The setup() function executes once when the Arduino starts or resets. It is used for one-time initialization, setting pin modes, starting serial communication, etc.

   A. repeat()
   B. loop()
   C. setup()
   D. execute()

.. mcq:: Which command would you MOST likely use to read a sensor value?
   :correct: B
   :explanation: analogRead() reads the voltage on an analog pin (A0-A5) and returns a value between 0 and 1023, corresponding to 0-5V. This is how biosignals from BioAmp boards are read.

   A. digitalWrite()
   B. analogRead()
   C. Serial.begin()
   D. tone()
